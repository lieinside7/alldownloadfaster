The tool is web-based, so there's no Dailymotion downloader app to install:

How to download a Dailymotion video by URL

  1. Open Dailymotion, find your video, and copy its link from the address bar or the Share button.
  2. Paste the URL into the box at the top of this page.
  3. Pick your quality (e.g. 1080p) or the MP3 option.
  4. Click Download and save the file.

Why a download might fail

  • Private or unlisted videos can't be fetched — only public ones.
  • Geo-restricted videos that Dailymotion blocks in some regions may not load.
  • If nothing happens, re-copy the full dailymotion.com/video/… link and try again.
