Where do my videos go to after they are downloaded?

Where your downloaded videos end up depends on your device and how you downloaded them. Here's a general guide:

  • Android Phones & Tablets: Downloaded videos usually go to a folder called "Downloads". Find this with your device's file manager app, named "Files" or "My Files" for example.
  • iPhones & iPads: Downloads depend on the app. For instance, videos downloaded from Safari might be in your camera roll, while PDFs from other apps might be stored within that app. There's no single "Downloads" folder on iPhones or iPads.
  • Computers: Downloaded files typically go to your Downloads folder. This is easy to find on most computers. On Windows PCs, it's usually a folder named "Downloads" in the left menu of File Explorer. On Macs, it's a folder named "Downloads" in your Dock.

Is it possible to download a private Vimeo video?

No, it's generally not possible to download a private Vimeo video unless you have explicit permission from the video's owner or if you are the owner yourself.

Attempting to download a private Vimeo video without proper authorization would be a violation of Vimeo's terms of service and could result in legal consequences. If you are the owner of the private video, you may have options to download it through your Vimeo account settings or by contacting Vimeo support for assistance. However, it's important to always respect the privacy settings and ownership rights of videos on Vimeo to avoid any legal issues.
