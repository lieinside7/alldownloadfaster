# NOTE: query-parameter URLs (?video=, ?download=, ?v=, old ?url=, spam params)
# are intentionally NOT blocked here. They carry a rel="canonical" pointing to the

# so crawlers must be ALLOWED to fetch them to read that canonical and consolidate /
# drop the duplicate. Blocking them with "Disallow: /*?" caused "indexed but blocked
# by robots.txt" — the URL stays indexed but the canonical is never seen. Do not re-add.
