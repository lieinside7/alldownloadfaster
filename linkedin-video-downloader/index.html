How to Download LinkedIn Videos

  1. Find the LinkedIn post: Open LinkedIn and locate the post or article containing the video you want to save.
  2. Copy the post URL: Click the three dots (⋯) on the post and select "Copy link to post". On mobile, tap Share then "Copy link".
  3. Paste the URL: Return to this page and paste the link into the input box at the top.
  4. Choose format: Select MP4 video or MP3 audio.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I download private LinkedIn videos?

No. Only publicly visible LinkedIn posts can be downloaded. Videos set to "Connections only" or "Private" are not accessible via URL-based tools. You must be able to view the video without logging in.

Can I download LinkedIn Learning course videos?

LinkedIn Learning course videos require a paid subscription and are protected. Only publicly accessible preview clips can be downloaded. Full course content is not supported.

Can I download LinkedIn Live recordings?

Yes, if the LinkedIn Live recording has been published as a public post after the event, you can download it using our tool.

Is downloading LinkedIn videos legal?

Downloading LinkedIn videos for personal, non-commercial reference is generally permitted. Do not redistribute or repurpose downloaded content without the creator's permission. Always respect LinkedIn's Terms of Service and the intellectual property of content creators.
